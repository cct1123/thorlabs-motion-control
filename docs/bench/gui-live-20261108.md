# Live web UI debugging: 8 November 2026

The production Dash UI controlled K10CR2 **55551174**, firmware **2.0.1**, through
our WinUSB/APT owner. Browser clicks, press/release actions and form edits initiated
the operations; no direct command script initiated motion. `record_gui.py` observed
requests, raw traffic and readbacks through that same owner. No Kinesis/runtime
module was imported. All recording sessions shut down and released their owner.

The operator initially permitted ±0.5° about the initial zero and excluded Home,
then expanded the envelope to ±20° and confirmed a USB supply above 0.9 A. EEPROM
saving remained unchecked. No Home or EEPROM packet appears in the captured traffic.

## Reproduced defects and fixes

1. **Connected hardware, frozen disconnected page.** Connect completed at zero,
   homed/enabled/idle, but status rendering was rejected. The migration check
   required a `state` list even when Dash omitted it because the callback had no
   State fields. Omitted empty lists are now accepted; old callback signatures
   remain rejected. Test requests now use the browser's omission behavior. The
   restarted browser displayed fresh identity, position, status and control states.
2. **Idle mount reported Moving after reaching near-zero.** At 20°/s and 20°/s²,
   the −12° to zero return stopped at **−1 count**, with status `0x80000400`.
   Exact target comparison kept the owner waiting until the operator Stop;
   the operation lasted 22.796 s despite idle readbacks. Absolute Move completion
   now accepts a residual of one count after fresh healthy idle/homed/enabled
   readback and progress. Home still requires exact zero. Repeating the same move
   again ended at −1 count and completed normally in **1.594 s**, without Stop or
   reconnect. The readout retains the actual count rather than snapping to target.

One count is about 0.000007324°. It is a software completion tolerance, not an
independent mechanical accuracy specification. Offline regressions cover both
residual signs, rejection of ±2-count residuals, stale/no-progress evidence, and
Home's unchanged exact-zero requirement.

The two 24° sweeps lasted 2.235 s and 2.422 s including preflight, polling and
completion handling. The largest sampled finite-difference counter rates were
18.968°/s forward and 17.838°/s reverse. These are software sample intervals;
they neither measure instantaneous peak speed nor calibrate physical motion.
The profile had enough nominal travel to attain 20°/s; short held controls did not.

## Bounds, restoration and final state

Recorded extents were −0.0999976° to +0.3500024° in the initial test and **−12° to
+12°** in the expanded test. Initial observer guards were ±0.4° / 1 s manual;
expanded guards were ±15° / 0.4 s manual. No observer guard fired. These counted
observers are not physical safety interlocks. At 20°/s and 20°/s², nominal stopping
distance is 10°, so maximum-profile held controls used only 200 ms presses near
zero; a 15° observer threshold alone would not guarantee the ±20° envelope.

The recorder keeps its 3° default; its explicit `--max-degrees` ceiling was expanded
from 3° to 20° for the operator-approved test. Bounds are centered on absolute
counted zero, not automatically on the current position. Inspect clearance,
baseline and braking margin before choosing an explicit bound.

After the final GUI owner closed, `restore_raw_settings.py` used its fresh
homed/idle/exact-zero prerequisite to restore velocity, Jog and backlash without
motion or EEPROM writes. Home and button groups were checked unchanged. All five
groups matched the captured baseline exactly. A fresh production GUI connection
again showed **0 counts**, `0x80000400`, no fault/recovery/UNKNOWN flag, and the
restored 10°/s, 10°/s² move profile. The final session retained the operator's supply
confirmation, then disconnected and released USB; the local web server remained
available for Connect.
